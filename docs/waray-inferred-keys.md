## What is empty, and why

- **Waray 30, screens 2 to 6.** This lesson is only the final exam. Its words, phrases and dialogues are the exam questions, so teaching them first would give away the answers.
- **729 of 1259 vocabulary cards have no example.** No sentence in that lesson uses the word, and no Waray may be invented. The card hides the example block.
- **Waray 30, Part F.** This is a practical speaking and writing task, so it stays unscored in `extraActivities`.
- **Audio.** No recordings exist yet, so all audio uses text-to-speech, as before.

## Also different from Tagalog Lesson 1

- **Screen titles.** The Usage and Patterns screens have fixed titles in their components: "Common greetings and their usage" and "Introduce yourself". Every Waray lesson shows them, including lessons on other topics.
- **XP.** The rows are the plan's own, totalling 60 XP. The plan has no speaking row in any lesson, so speaking earns no XP in Waray.
- **Language notes** have their own screen after Usage, which Tagalog Lesson 1 does not have. They come straight from the plan, so this adds one screen to Waray 1–29.
- **Waray 4–8 assessment.** The plan gives 3 options per question. The fourth is a wrong option taken from another question in the same assessment.
- **Waray 14–17 assessment.** The plan has open questions with written answers. They became multiple choice: the plan's answer is the key, the wrong options come from the same lesson, and the 70% pass mark is inferred.
- **Conversations.** Most lessons have 3 or 4, shown one at a time on the same screen, because the plan has that many.

## How the values were made

- **Pronunciation:** the plan's own respelling when any Waray lesson gives one for that word. Otherwise syllables are split by rule. On words the plan respells, the rule matches 95% (751 of 790).
- **Usage line:** from the title of the list the word is in.
- **Examples:** the first unused conversation exchange in the lesson that has the word, otherwise a phrase from the lesson.
- **Target pitch:** high for phrases ending in ? or !, otherwise medium.
- **Target mood:** from the English meaning (for example "thank" is happy, "sorry" is sad), otherwise neutral. Mood is only a hint and never affects scores.
- **Badge names:** from the plan. Only the description sentence is inferred.
